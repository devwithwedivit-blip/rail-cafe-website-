The Heritage Journey

Step Aboard Two Authentic Rail Coaches

Where the grand golden era of Indian Railways meets modern gourmet fine dining.

Conceived as a tribute to India’s legendary railway history, THE RAIL CAFE spans two real, retired broad-gauge railway coaches permanently stationed on gleaming steel tracks. Meticulously restored with handcrafted teak and mahogany wood paneling, polished brass luggage racks, and plush emerald velvet booths, every inch evokes the romance of a cross-country luxury express.
